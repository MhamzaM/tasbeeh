# Privacy Policy — Tasbeeh

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Tasbeeh is a free dhikr counter by MhamzaM (FreeApps by Hamza).

- The app **does not collect, upload or share any personal data.** No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The app **has no internet permission**, so nothing can leave your device.
- Your counts, history, custom adhkar and settings are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- The only permission is **vibration**, for feedback while counting.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MhamzaM/tasbeeh/issues
